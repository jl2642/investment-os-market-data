# FMDL-6X1-A Change Control

Any later change to the dual activation model requires an explicit change-control record.

A future US brokerage channel may update only the Brokerage & Real-Account Gate and channel-specific constraints. It must not retroactively alter accepted research evidence, Security IDs, source lineage or prior immutable releases.

A source-route or identity defect may trigger one of the two permitted targeted repair rounds. Cosmetic renaming, broader feature requests and unbounded phase splitting are not repair grounds.

The Research Production Gate can authorize only the next formally accepted research-production phase. It cannot independently mutate Investment OS state.
