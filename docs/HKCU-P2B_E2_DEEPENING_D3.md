# HKCU P2B-E2 — Company Evidence Deepening D3

D3 continues accepted D2 by targeting only the **14 HIGH-materiality graduation blockers** identified in P2A ranks 1–20. It is not a new screening round and does not force all `EVIDENCE_PARTIAL` rows to `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`.

## Scope and method

The 14 rows span 9 securities. Each row is re-tested against current primary HKEX issuer evidence and receives one of four decision states:

- `CLEARED_MONITOR` — the original blocker is sufficiently resolved; retain monitoring only.
- `RECLASSIFIED_MONITOR` — the original blocker was over-broad or misclassified; retain monitoring only.
- `RETAINED_TARGETED` — the blocker remains materially unresolved.
- `RECLASSIFIED_TARGETED` — the original classification changes, but a material decision blocker remains.

No alpha score is assigned. No formal Candidate graduation is permitted.

## Decision result

D3 resolves 9 of the 14 HIGH blockers to monitoring status and retains 5 for targeted work. Together with the 7 MEDIUM blockers deliberately left untouched, the top-20 queue falls from 21 blockers to **12 blockers across 10 securities**. Ten of the top 20 therefore become `READY_WITH_CONFIDENCE_CAP`; ten remain `TARGETED_DEEPENING_REQUIRED`.

## Evidence discipline

A newly discovered cross-dimension signal cannot be silently discarded merely because the original blocker category changes. This is why the Brilliance China fresh profit warning remains an explicit blocker and why the Guangdong Investment FY2025 alert/Q1 2026 evidence is reconciled rather than converted into an unsupported positive 2026 revision claim.
