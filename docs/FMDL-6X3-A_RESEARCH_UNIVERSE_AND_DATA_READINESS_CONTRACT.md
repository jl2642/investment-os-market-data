# FMDL-6X3-A — Research Universe & Data Readiness Contract

This stage converts the accepted US data stores into a fully profiled research universe and an explicit per-Security/per-Issuer readiness matrix.

It does **not** calculate normalized financials, factors, valuation scores, rankings or Investment OS candidates. A Security can be in the research universe while remaining blocked by SEC facts, market-history or instrument-profile gaps.

The accepted output opens FMDL-6X3-B only for Securities whose supported research profile is backed by official SEC facts. Yahoo market history remains non-decision-grade and may support sandbox research only.
