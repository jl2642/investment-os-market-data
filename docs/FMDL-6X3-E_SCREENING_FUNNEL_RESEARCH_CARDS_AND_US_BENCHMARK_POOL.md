# FMDL-6X3-E｜Screening Funnel, Research Cards & US Benchmark Pool

## Objective

Create a full-universe evidence-gated screening state, one auditable Research Card for every Canonical Security, and a small US research-validation benchmark pool without mutating the Investment OS candidate pool.

## Accepted first baseline

- All 8,785 Canonical Securities receive exactly one screening disposition and one Research Card.
- The funnel is gate-based and non-ranked; missing data creates explicit blockers rather than neutral scores.
- AAPL, MSFT and NVDA form the core quarterly-quality and market/benchmark sandbox.
- JPM, BRK.B and XOM form an official-filing and classification watch layer while SEC facts and special-profile normalization remain pending.
- QQQ is retained only as the Nasdaq-100 reference instrument.
- The seven-member `US_RESEARCH_BENCHMARK_POOL_V1` is a research-validation pool, not an Investment OS candidate pool.
- Market and benchmark-relative observations remain `NON_DECISION_GRADE_FALLBACK`.
- Formal valuation, formal peer ranks, global scores, candidate promotion, simulation and trading remain closed.

## Completion boundary

This stage accepts the screening, card and benchmark-pool production system. It does not claim complete research readiness, a formal stock ranking, an investment recommendation or candidate-pool authorization.
