# FMDL-6X2-FINAL — Full Store Reconciliation & Operational Acceptance

This phase accepts the five FMDL-6X2 production stores as a coherent, resumable US data foundation. Acceptance does not imply that all historical listing, market or SEC records have already been backfilled.

The reconciled identity chain is 8,807 Listings, 8,785 Securities and 7,419 Issuers. The accepted market store contains a 64-security dual-route baseline and an explicit remaining queue. The accepted SEC store contains six filing-linked issuers, three fact issuers and an explicit remaining issuer queue.

The exit opens FMDL-6X3 research-model production only. Candidate Pool, simulation, real-account, brokerage and order gates remain closed.
