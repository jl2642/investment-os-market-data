# FMDL-6X3 and FMDL-6X4 Development Roadmap

## FMDL-6X3 — US Research Production, Factor & Benchmark Pool

- **6X3-A** — Research Universe & Data Readiness Contract
- **6X3-B** — Financial Normalization, TTM & Annual Metric Layer
- **6X3-C** — Factor, Valuation, Quality & Risk Engine
- **6X3-D** — Sector, Industry, Peer & Benchmark Framework
- **6X3-E** — Screening Funnel, Research Cards & US Benchmark Pool
- **6X3-FINAL** — Research Production Reconciliation & Acceptance

6X3 produces a research benchmark pool. It does not directly mutate the Investment OS Candidate Pool.

## FMDL-6X4 — Public Equity Investing & Investment OS Integration

- **6X4-A** — Public Equity Investing Adapter & Contract Mapping
- **6X4-B** — Research Workflow Integration & Evidence Registration
- **6X4-C** — Candidate Graduation, Decision Interface & Guardrails
- **6X4-D** — Simulation-only Pilot, Attribution & Failure Recovery
- **6X4-E** — Cross-market Comparability & Operating Runbook
- **6X4-FINAL** — US Research Adapter Operational Acceptance & FMDL-6 Freeze

FMDL-6 is complete only after 6X4-FINAL accepts X1–X4 and freezes the US research adapter. Completion does not require a brokerage channel; the brokerage and real-account gate may remain closed. FMDL-7 then performs cross-market and full-system final operational acceptance.
