# FMDL-2B-4 Non-claims

FMDL-2B-4 maintains history and factor evidence. It does not:

- demonstrate that any factor produces alpha;
- define or authorize screening sleeves;
- create a new Longlist or candidate pool;
- upgrade a research rank into BUY, ADD or SELL permission;
- alter the simulation account or real account;
- establish survivorship-free point-in-time backtest validity;
- introduce financial, valuation, market-cap, dividend or analyst factors;
- connect to a broker or execute an order.
