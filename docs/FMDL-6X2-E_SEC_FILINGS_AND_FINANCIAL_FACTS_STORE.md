# FMDL-6X2-E — SEC Filings & Financial Facts Store

## Execution boundary

GitHub-hosted runners returned HTTP 403 for the SEC ticker map, nightly submissions ZIP and nightly companyfacts ZIP. Decision-grade SEC evidence therefore enters through a controlled ChatGPT Web official-retrieval bundle and is independently hash-validated and parsed in GitHub. Third-party SEC proxies remain forbidden.

## Initial baseline

The first Release binds six official 2026 Form 10-Q filings. AAPL, MSFT and NVDA include normalized income-statement facts with taxonomy, unit, period and accession lineage. JPM, BRK.B and XOM establish official CIK and filing metadata. All remaining Canonical Issuers are retained in a machine-readable backfill queue.

## Completion claim

This stage accepts the production mechanism and an official partial evidence baseline. It does not claim full-universe or full-history SEC completion. Amendments and later accessions must remain separate; missing facts cannot be neutral-filled.
